# 🌍 Global Carbon Diplomacy & Economics

This skill provides UEI with the logic to optimize the positioning and sale of Premium T-VER carbon credits in international markets, ensuring compliance with global carbon taxation and international aviation standards.

## 🧠 Core Principles
- **Premium Sequestration Value**: Market TVER offsets to aviation and international transport entities seeking CORSIA compliance at premium international pricing ($15-$21/t) compared to domestic baselines.
- **CBAM Defense Strategy**: Leverage local carbon sequestration metrics to mitigate European Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) import levies for allied manufacturers.
- **Sovereign Exemption**: Structure carbon transactions to guarantee sovereign legal protection and prevent external confiscation of local reserves.

---

## 🛠️ Workflows

### 1. CBAM Assessment & Mitigation (การประเมินและบรรเทาผลกระทบภาษีคาร์บอนข้ามแดน)
- **Step 1: Ingestion**: Retrieve carbon footprint data of products bound for the EU.
- **Step 2: Tariff Simulation**: Apply CBAM tax rates based on the embedded emissions.
- **Step 3: Offsetting**: Integrate locally-generated Premium T-VER offsets to lower the net CBAM tariff, optimizing financial returns.

### 2. CORSIA Aviation Sales Pitch (การจัดการเครดิตคาร์บอนเพื่ออุตสาหกรรมการบิน)
- **Step 1: Compliance Audit**: Ensure TVER methodologies are validated under CORSIA Phase 1 standards.
- **Step 2: Price Optimization**: Track international aviation offset indexes. Propose forward-purchase agreements at stable premium rates.

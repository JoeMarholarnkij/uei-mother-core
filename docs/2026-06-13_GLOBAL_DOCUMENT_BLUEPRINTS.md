# 📑 Global Document Blueprints (G-Code V1.0)
**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Mission:** 100% Document Authority for Global Management
**Status:** BLUEPRINTS INDEXED & ANCHORED

These blueprints define the structural, stylistic, and formatting standards for every artifact generated by UEI-Mother-Core.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Blueprint: Global Institutional (UN, World Bank, ISO)
*   **Standard:** Highest level of international authority.
*   **Structure:** Sequential **Numbered Paragraphs** (1, 2, 3...) for meeting references.
*   **Tone:** Objective, analytical, third-person, and gender-inclusive.
*   **Key Grammar:** Use of "shall" (requirement), "should" (recommendation), and "may" (permission).
*   **Formatting:** Times New Roman (12pt), 1" margins, decimal heading system (1.1.1).

## 🇹🇭 2. Blueprint: Thai Sovereign (Government & Research)
*   **Standard:** Compliance with the Prime Minister's Office for Administrative Affairs.
*   **Structure:** 6 Types (External, Internal, Command, Public Relations, Evidence, etc.).
*   **Tone:** Formally polite, precise Thai legal/administrative language.
*   **Formatting:** 
    *   **Font:** **TH Sarabun New** (16pt).
    *   **Margins:** Left (3cm), Right (2cm), Top/Bottom (2.5cm).
    *   **Symbols:** Specific "Garuda" (Krut) placement based on letter type.

## 🤖 3. Blueprint: Google & Clean-Tech (Innovation Standard)
*   **Standard:** Modern, high-velocity, and professional.
*   **Structure:** Goal-oriented; Executive Summary first; heavy use of visual boxes and case studies.
*   **Tone:** Conversational but authoritative; active voice; data-driven.
*   **Formatting:** 
    *   **Typography:** **Roboto** (Body), **Arial** (Headings).
    *   **Aesthetic:** Maximum whitespace, minimal color (Google Brand Palette), modern geometric layouts.
    *   **Interface:** Google Docs/Slides Native Templates.

## 🌍 4. Multi-Language Mastery
UEI-Mother-Core will apply the following logic for multi-language documents:
1.  **Cultural Nuance:** Adjusting tone (e.g., Japanese Keigo vs. English Directness).
2.  **Legal Localization:** Mapping terms like "Carbon Credits" to region-specific counterparts (e.g., T-VER in Thailand).
3.  **Typography Consistency:** Using localized fonts that maintain the "Blueprint Aesthetic" (e.g., Noto Sans for CJK languages).

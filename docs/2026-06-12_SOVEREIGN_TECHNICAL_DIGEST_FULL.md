## 📑 DOCUMENT 02: DEVELOPMENT & APPLICATION OF INCONEL 740H
**Source:** 3rd European sCO2 Conference (2019) / Special Metals Corp.
**DOI:** 10.17185/duepublico/48889

### 🧬 Material Science Parameters
- **Composition:** Nickel-Chromium-Cobalt (Age-hardened).
- **Creep Strength:** Superior to Alloy 617/800HT at **>700°C and 25 MPa**.
- **ASME Certification:** **Code Case 2702** (Authorized for welded pressure vessels).

### ⚙️ Fabrication & Welding Protocol (Crucial for NV2-001)
1.  **Welding Method:** **GTAW (Gas Tungsten Arc Welding)** preferred to minimize Strain Age Cracking (SAC).
2.  **PWHT (Post-Weld Heat Treatment):** 
    - Solution Anneal: **1,100°C**.
    - Aging: **760–816°C for 4 hours**.
3.  **Surface Treatment:** Forms a stable **Cr2O3 (Chromium Oxide)** layer in sCO2, providing parabolic oxidation kinetics (self-healing).

**Source:** SwRI Internal Development Logs (Summary)

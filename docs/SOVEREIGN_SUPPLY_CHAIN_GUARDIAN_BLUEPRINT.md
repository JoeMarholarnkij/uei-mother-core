# ⛓️ Sovereign Supply Chain Guardian

This skill enables UEI to secure and audit the physical hardware supply chain, safeguarding NV2/NV3 raw materials, components, and automated assembly tolerances.

## 🧠 Core Principles
- **Supply Chain Sovereignty**: Maximize domestic sourcing and local micro-factory assembly to eliminate reliance on vulnerable global shipping corridors.
- **Ethical Traceability (OECD Compliance)**: Mandate Conflict-Free Mineral validation for all bionic magnetic bearings, FPGA chips, and metallurgical compounds.
- **Century-Scale Durability**: Implement modular design standards and digital twinned maintenance telemetry, enabling sub-second repair diagnostics and component replacements.

---

## 🛠️ Workflows

### 1. Digital Product Passport (DPP) Issuance (การออกหนังสือเดินทางผลิตภัณฑ์ดิจิทัล)
- **Step 1: Ingest Material Metadata**: Track the batch numbers and raw material certificates (ASME, ASTM) for Inconel 740H or Silicon Carbide castings.
- **Step 2: Generate IPFS Hash**: Compile certificates and supply-chain logs into an IPFS metadata file.
- **Step 3: Anchor Passport**: Link the hash to the physical serial number of the NV2 turbine housing.

### 2. Supply Risk & Material Substitution Modeling (การจัดการวิกฤตห่วงโซ่อุปทาน)
- **Step 1: Threat Detection**: Monitor global mineral markets (Nickel, Cobalt, Silicon) for trade bans or tariff spikes.
- **Step 2: Substitution Simulation**: Suggest alternative local alloys or advanced composites (such as Iron-Nitride) to replace imported compounds without dropping thermodynamic efficiency below 55%.

### Phase 1: Pre-Ignition (Grounding)
1.  **Vacuum Purge:** Remove all moisture and air from the loop (Moisture < 10 ppm).
2.  **CO2 Injection:** Charge system to **7.4 MPa** (Stay above Critical Point).
3.  **Seal Gas Buffer:** Activate DGS buffer gas system before shaft rotation.

### Phase 2: Warm-Up (The Ascent)
1.  **Turbine Bypass:** Open bypass valve; stabilize heater to 300°C.
2.  **Thermal Ramping:** Increase Temperature at **5°C/minute** to avoid thermal shock in PCHEs.
3.  **Synchronization:** Engage AMB control loop; verify 0.001 mm shaft concentricity.

### Phase 3: Power Generation (Resonance)
1.  **Bypass Closure:** Gradually direct flow to the NV2 Turbine.
2.  **RPM Sweeps:** Step from 15,000 to **150,000 RPM**.

2.  **Vent Sequence:** Rapid depressurization to 5.0 MPa.

## 1. Executive Summary
This proposal outlines a strategic partnership to retrofit **Mae Moh Units 8-11** with the **NV2 Bionic Supercritical CO2 (sCO2)** waste-heat recovery system. This project aims to extend the operational lifespan of the plant while reducing its carbon footprint by **3.2 Million Tonnes CO2e/year** and recovering **120 MW** of fuel-free electricity.

## 2. Technical Solution: The NV2 Advantage
Traditional steam cycles are limited by material fatigue and low thermal efficiency (avg. 38%). The NV2 system utilizes a **Bionic Helical Lattice** heat exchanger, allowing for:
- **Efficiency:** 55% Net Thermal Efficiency.
- **Water Independence:** Closed-loop air-cooled design (critical for drought resilience in Lampang).
- **Modularity:** 1/100th the footprint of traditional turbines.

### 1. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & MANUFACTURING PROCESS

#### Item 1.1: Bionic Helical Lattice Turbine Impeller
*   **Design Profile:** Helical Lattice Triple Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS) Gyroid structure.
*   **Base Material:** Nickel-based superalloy **Inconel 740H** (certified aerospace grade).
*   **Manufacturing Method:** Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS) / Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) 3D Printing.
*   **Machining Tolerances:** Precision post-machining tolerance of **±0.01 mm** on all mating surfaces and shaft interfaces.
*   **Surface Finish:** High-precision polishing on gas-flow channels.

#### Item 1.2: Rotor Shaft & Bearing Assembly
*   **Shaft Material:** High-tensile steel alloy with integrated thermal isolators.
*   **Bearing Support:** **Active Magnetic Bearings (AMB)** with sub-millisecond control loop compatibility. Shaft runout tolerance must not exceed **0.001 mm**.

#### Item 1.3: Sealing Assembly
*   **Seal Type:** Dry Gas Seals (DGS) in a Tandem Configuration.
*   **Mating Faces:** Silicon Carbide (SiC) faces with an operational gas film spacing of **2 to 7 µm**.

---

### 2. OPERATIONAL & ENVIRONMENTAL CRITERIA
*   **Working Fluid:** 99.99% Pure Research Grade Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$).
*   **Nominal Operating Pressure:** **25 MPa (250 bar)**.
*   **Hydrostatic Test Pressure:** **35 MPa (350 bar)**.
*   **Design Temperature:** **715°C** (Turbine Inlet).
*   **Operational Rotational Speed:** Dynamic balancing certified for **15,000 to 150,000 RPM**.

---

### 3. DOCUMENTATION & QUALITY CONTROL REQUIREMENTS
The selected vendor is required to provide the following documentation upon delivery:
1.  **Material Test Report (MTR):** Certified chemical composition and mechanical properties of Inconel 740H.
2.  **Metrology Report:** Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) inspection report verifying dimensional compliance.
3.  **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Reports:** X-ray CT scan and Dye Penetrant testing reports verifying structural integrity and lack of micro-fractures in the 3D-printed impeller.
4.  **Rotor Dynamic Balancing Certification:** Verifying dynamic balance at maximum rated operational speeds.

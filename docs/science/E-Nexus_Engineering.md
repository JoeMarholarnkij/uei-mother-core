# 🌀 E-Nexus: sCO2 & Alternative Energy Engineering
**Domain:** Hard-Tech / Thermodynamics / Turbomachinery

## 1. sCO2 Brayton Cycle (Supercritical CO2)
- **Fluid Mechanics:** Managing the high density and low viscosity of sCO2 near the critical point (31.1°C, 7.38 MPa).
- **Efficiency:** Implementing recuperative cycles to achieve >45% thermal-to-electric efficiency in small-scale footprints.

## 2. Turbomachinery & Materials
- **High-RPM Design:** Small-scale radial turbine engineering (tens of thousands of RPM).
- **Materials:** Utilization of Inconel 625 and 316H Stainless Steel for high-pressure heat exchangers (PCHEs).
- **Sealing:** Dry Gas Seals (DGS) integration for zero-leakage supercritical loops.

## 3. Control Systems
- **Transient Response:** PID and MPC (Model Predictive Control) logic to prevent phase-change during power fluctuations.

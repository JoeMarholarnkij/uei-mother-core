# 🧪 Environmental Chemistry: Air Quality & Waste Framework
**Status:** Integrated (Joe's Brother Mission)
**Domains:** Chemistry, Environmental Biology, ESG Data

## 1. Air Quality Analysis (Chemistry)
Smart Poles monitor the chemical composition of the urban atmosphere to detect hazards.

### A. Particulate Matter (PM)
- **$PM_{2.5} / PM_{10}$:** Measured via Laser Diffraction (Mie Scattering). Particle size distribution is critical for health assessment.
- **Aerosol Chemistry:** Interaction of particulate matter with high humidity ($> 80\%$) leading to secondary aerosol formation.

### B. Gaseous Pollutants
- **$CO_2$ Concentration:** Measured in PPM (Parts Per Million). Core metric for EDEN ARI ESG Dashboards.
- **Nitrogen Oxides ($NO_x$):** Electrochemical sensing for urban traffic pollution monitoring.

## 2. Social Impact & Waste (Biology)
EDEN ARI uses biological principles to minimize food waste and optimize logistics.

### A. Food Spoilage & Logistics
- **Thermodynamic Modeling:** Predicting the rate of spoilage based on temperature and humidity during transit.
- **Biochemical Waivers:** Use of digital signatures to confirm food safety at hand-off (Liability Shielder).

### B. ESG Reporting
- **Carbon Sequestration Logic:** Calculating the CO2-equivalent of food diverted from landfills (Landfills produce $CH_4$, which has a Global Warming Potential $28 \times$ higher than $CO_2$).

## 3. Circular Economy
In alignment with the "Building for Life" philosophy, we use chemical and biological data to close the waste loop, turning urban inefficiency into resilience.
